Good to know

Common questions

How long does the matching process take? +

We typically move from the initial interview to deployment within a few weeks, depending on the speed of documentation and embassy endorsements.

What is the Maid Loan process? +

We advance the placement costs so your initial cash flow stays protected, and the loan is repaid gradually via salary deductions over the agreed term.

Can I interview candidates online? +

Yes — we arrange secure, home-based video interviews so you can shortlist and speak with candidates without visiting an office.

Do you help with the Employer Orientation Program (EOP)? +

Yes. Our team guides first-time employers through the EOP requirement along with the rest of the MOM, ICA and embassy paperwork.
