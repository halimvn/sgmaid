About Us

At SG Maid Agency, we believe finding a helper is a deeply personal family matter. Our founder comes from a lineage of care, with over two decades of experience.

Whether you are preparing your home for Ramadan or ensuring your elderly parents are well-cared for, we understand the cultural nuances that make a house a home. Because we’ve seen it all over the last 24 years, we are uniquely equipped to mediate complex household dynamics that leave newer agencies overwhelmed.
